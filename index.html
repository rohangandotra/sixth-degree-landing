FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Email Aaryan and Rohan. We answer everything.

hello@sixthdegree.app
What does Sixth Degree cost?

Creators keep 100% of their quoted fee. Brands pay a 10% platform fee on top when they fund by pre-authorized debit from a Canadian bank account, or 12.4% by card. The fee is shown before you fund a collaboration.

What if the creator never posts?

Nothing is paid out until the post is live. If no content has been submitted by the end of the campaign's posting window, the full amount, platform fee included, is refunded to you automatically. Anything else goes through a dispute, and a dispute pauses release until it's resolved.

What if the post doesn't perform?

The fee pays for the work, not for a result, so we never promise numbers. What you get is a recap that shows what the post did, with every figure labelled by who measured it, so you can decide whether to hire that creator again.

How are creators verified?

Verification is a human decision, not an algorithm's guess. A person checks that the account is real and belongs to the creator, that their photo is them, that they're based in the Greater Toronto Area, and that their content is something a brand would be happy with. Every creator is due for a fresh review every 60 days. Brands can only hire creators who pass.

What's the difference between nano and micro creators?

Follower band. Nano creators usually have a few thousand followers; micro creators reach into the tens of thousands. Both trade raw reach for trust: smaller audiences that actually know them. On Sixth Degree you can filter by follower band, niche, neighbourhood, and rate.

Where do the campaign ideas come from?

From public posts by Toronto accounts in the niches we cover. Each post is ranked against what that account usually gets, so a big account doesn't win just for being big, and every idea cites the posts it came from, each with the date we read it. Nothing in it is a promise of results.

Can I sign up today?

Creators, yes: create your profile and we'll verify your account. Brands join by invite while we onboard our first campaigns. Join the waitlist and we'll reach out.

Do you only work with Toronto brands?

Toronto is where we're starting, on purpose. The creators live here and so do their audiences. We're Canadian-first, and once Canada is won, Brazil and Colombia are next.
