04°

Questions, answered.

How does escrow protect brands and creators?+

Brands fund the full fee to escrow before work starts, so creators never chase invoices. Creators keep 100% of their quoted fee. Funds are released when the brand confirms the post is live, or automatically after 3 business days if the brand takes no action. If there's a dispute, release pauses until it's resolved.

How are creators verified?+

Verification is a human decision, not an algorithm's guess. A person checks that the account is real and belongs to the creator, that their photo is them, that they're based in the Greater Toronto Area, and that their content is something a brand would be happy with. Every creator is due for a fresh review every 60 days. Brands can only hire creators who pass. The platform enforces it.

Where do the campaign ideas come from?+

From public posts by Toronto accounts in the niches we cover. Each post is ranked against what that account usually gets, so a big account doesn't win just for being big, and every idea cites the posts it came from, each with the date we read it. AI drafts a brief from the idea you pick, and you edit it before anything goes out. Nothing in it is a promise of results.

What does Sixth Degree cost?+

Creators keep 100% of their quoted fee. Brands pay a platform fee on top, shown before you fund a collaboration. We haven't published standard pricing yet. Email hello@sixthdegree.app and we'll walk you through it.

What's the difference between nano and micro creators?+

Follower band. Nano creators usually have a few thousand followers; micro creators reach into the tens of thousands. Both trade raw reach for trust: smaller audiences that actually know them. On Sixth Degree you can filter by follower band, niche, neighbourhood, and rate.

Can I sign up today?+

Creators, yes: create your profile and we'll verify your account. Brands join by invite while we onboard our first campaigns. Join the waitlist and we'll reach out.

Do you only work with Toronto brands?+

Toronto is where we're starting, on purpose. The creators live here and so do their audiences. That local context is not something a US platform can fake. We're Canadian-first, and once Canada is won, Brazil and Colombia are next.

How do brands know a campaign actually worked?+

Every collaboration gets a trackable link, plus a promo code if you set one up. What they trace is a verifiable floor, not a complete count. Some customers see a post and just walk in. Every number is labeled with its source, so you always know what's measured and what isn't.
