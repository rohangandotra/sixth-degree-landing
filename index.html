05°

Questions, answered.

How does escrow protect brands and creators?+

Brands fund the full fee to escrow before work starts, so creators never chase invoices. Creators keep 100% of their quoted fee. Funds are released when the brand confirms the post is live — or automatically after 3 business days if the brand takes no action. If there's a dispute, release pauses until it's resolved.

How are creators verified?+

Verification is a human decision, not an algorithm's guess. We check that the engagement is genuine and the audience is made of real people, then re-check every creator every 90 days. Brands can only hire creators who pass — the platform enforces it.

What does Sixth Degree cost?+

Creators keep 100% of their quoted fee. Brands pay a platform fee on top, shown before you fund a collaboration. We haven't published standard pricing yet — email hello@sixthdegree.app and we'll walk you through it.

What's the difference between nano and micro creators?+

Follower band. Nano creators usually have a few thousand followers; micro creators reach into the tens of thousands. Both trade raw reach for trust — smaller audiences that know them. On Sixth Degree you can filter by follower band, niche, neighbourhood, and rate.

Do you only work with Toronto brands?+

Toronto is where we're starting, on purpose. The creators live here and so do their audiences — context a US platform can't fake. We're Canadian-first, and once Canada is won, Brazil and Colombia are next.

When does Sixth Degree launch?+

Private beta opens Fall 2026. We're talking to brands and creators across Toronto right now — join the waitlist or email us and we'll be in touch.

How do brands know a campaign actually worked?+

Every creator gets trackable links and promo codes. What they trace is a verifiable floor, not a complete count — some customers see a post and just walk in. Every number is labeled with its source, so you always know what's measured and what isn't.
